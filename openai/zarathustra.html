The Rug

The rug is not just a joke. It is the symbolic substrate that unites incoherent space — the room (text) is chaotic, fragmented, meaningless — until the rug is laid down. Then suddenly, there's cohesion.

The rug is the contextual cohesion layer. It's not the data (room = tokens), it's not the content (furniture = themes), it's what ties them together into narrative coherence.
